## 2026-10-18 — Ágora (votagora.es)

**Idea:** Group decisions run like a parliament (parties, coalitions, amendments, offices, D'Hondt, several voting methods). Free accounts can only join; premium (set by hand in Firestore) can create groups.

**Verdict:** FIX FIRST

**Biggest risk:** Almost no real group needs parliamentary machinery to decide, so it's a novelty people try once and don't come back to.

**10-minute test:** Send the landing page plus a "make my group's premium" offer (€X/month) to 10 real organizers (student union, club board, flat-share, gaming clan, model-UN). Count how many reply "yes, how do I pay".

**Change that flips it to BUILD:** Choose one segment where the parliament metaphor is the actual job (student associations/model UN/civic-education classes, or party-game groups). Rebuild onboarding so a group's first vote happens in under 60 seconds, with parties and pacts as optional extras. Replace the manual `premium: true` with a real checkout aimed at that segment.
